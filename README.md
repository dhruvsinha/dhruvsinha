### Paricharcha Education Foundation
In 2023, I founded [Paricharcha Education Foundation](https://paricharchaedu.com/), a non-profit registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act in India. Paricharcha, Sanskrit for "discussion," helps children think critically, speak confidently, and catch up on foundational skills. I design the curriculum and teach classes in Hindi, built around a six-step framework: observe, reflect, express, create, reason, and lead. We partner with schools and grassroots organizations, where an on-ground teacher and digital infrastructure connect students with volunteer educators in live online sessions. Since 2024, we have worked with five partner organizations across India, including a government school in rural Uttar Pradesh and organizations supporting children rescued from child labor, and we currently teach about 200 children every week.
